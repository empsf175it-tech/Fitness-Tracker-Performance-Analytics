Data Governance & Ethics

Biometric Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 9, 2026 • Version 2.4

1. Absolute Athlete Data Ownership

At PulseForge, we operate on a single core principle: your physiological telemetry belongs exclusively to you. We do not sell, rent, monetize, or broker personal cardiovascular, nutritional, or sleep data to insurers, advertising brokers, or third-party algorithms.

2. Telemetry Categories Collected

When utilizing PulseForge devices and application integrations, we securely record:

  • Cardiovascular telemetry: Resting Heart Rate, Max Heart Rate, and Heart Rate Variability (rMSSD).
  • Mechanical training workload: Duration, categories, power output (Watts), and calculated Day Strain.
  • Metabolic intake: Macronutrient breakdown (proteins, carbohydrates, lipids) and cellular fluid volume.
  • Circadian sleep cycles: Stage 3/4 Deep Slow-Wave sleep, Rapid Eye Movement (REM), and latency indicators.

3. Coach & Client Access Control Isolation

Under our Client Access Control system, your assigned coach or clinic director can only access data if you have explicitly linked accounts. You hold the unilateral right to switch your coach permissions between "Edit" and "View-Only", or revoke coach access entirely at any second with instant enforcement.

4. Military-Grade Encryption Standards

All records are encrypted at rest using AES-256 GCM authenticated encryption. Data in transit is protected via TLS 1.3 cryptographic tunnels. Authentication credentials utilize salted bcrypt hashing with high work factors. Session tokens are preserved inside secured httpOnly cookies with strict SameSite protections.

5. Full Data Portability & The Right to Be Forgotten

You may export your complete, uncompressed historical dataset in CSV format at any time using our built-in export engine. If you decide to close your account, all associated database records and telemetry logs are permanently purged from our storage within 72 hours.

Questions or GDPR requests? Transmit inquiries directly to: privacy@pulseforge.io.
